WTW Privacy Policy

Effective [[EFFECTIVE DATE]]

WTW (What's the Word?) helps groups choose where to eat or what to do. This policy explains what WTW collects, why, who can see it and how to delete it. WTW is operated by [[OPERATOR NAME]] ("we"). Contact us at [[CONTACT EMAIL]].

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, display name and username. Your email is used for sign-in and is not shown to other people.
  • Profile and preferences: favorite cuisine and activity, Discover preferences, saved places and your profile sharing setting.
  • Social connections: friend requests and friendships, groups and group invitations, and people you block.
  • Things you write: group and direct messages, Group Ideas, hang posts, group names, custom plan options and notes, Pick cancel reasons and reports you submit.
  • Pick activity: Picks you host or join, your swipes, votes, RSVPs, hang responses and "Did you go?" answers.
  • Location: if you allow it, your device's location while you use the app, to center a Pick or Discover near you. We only store a location when a host chooses it as a Pick's center.
  • Device and technical data: a push notification token if you allow notifications, your in-app notifications, and records of actions used to limit abuse, such as attempts to join with a code.

We don't collect your contacts or birthday, and you can't upload photos. WTW has no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. We don't sell your data or use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use it

We use this data to run WTW's features, including sign-in, Picks, chats and notifications, and to keep the service safe by limiting abuse and reviewing reports.

Who can see it

  • People in a Pick: your display name and participation. Your individual swipes and final votes aren't shown to them, though results and group counts are.
  • Friends: your name and username. Counts, favorites, mutual groups and Past Moves appear only if you turn on profile sharing, which is off by default.
  • Group members and chat participants: what you post in that group or conversation.
  • Our team: reports, including a copy of a reported message, to review them and act on abuse.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server functions.
  • [[EMAIL PROVIDER]] delivers sign-in codes, so it receives your email address.
  • Expo, Apple and Google deliver push notifications using your device's push token. Message notifications don't contain the message text.
  • Google Places provides place results in Discover. Our server sends it your search area, without your name or account details.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists, unless you delete or change it sooner. Some technical records, such as resolved invitations and abuse-limit records, have no scheduled cleanup yet and remain until your account is deleted.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Profile or Settings. This removes your account, profile, friendships, preferences, saved places, votes, messages you sent, your hang posts and the groups you own. Some shared history can remain so that other people's records stay intact:

  • Completed Picks you hosted stay without your name, shown as "Deleted user".
  • Text you added to a Pick's options, such as a custom plan option or a Group Idea used in a Pick, can stay in that Pick for other participants, without your name.

Deleting your account also deletes reports you made and reports naming you.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, turn profile sharing off, block people, and turn off location or notifications in your device settings. WTW still works without them, though you may need to choose locations yourself. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights, such as asking for a copy of your data. Contact us to make a request.

Security

We limit access to your data with database access rules and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

WTW isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date, and tell you in the app if the changes are significant.
