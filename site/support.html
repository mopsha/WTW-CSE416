WTW Support

Need help with WTW? Email [[CONTACT EMAIL]] and include the email you sign in with, if you can. Please don't send your sign-in code.

Common questions

I didn't get my sign-in code

Check your spam folder, and wait a minute before requesting another code.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the "…" on a person or message, then choose Report or Block. You can unblock people in Settings.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile and tap Delete account. The Privacy Policy explains what's removed.

Why does WTW ask for my location?

To center a Pick or Discover near you. It's optional, and you can change it in your device settings.
